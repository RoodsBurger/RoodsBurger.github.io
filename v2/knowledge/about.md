## Who Rodolfo is

Rodolfo Raimundo is a machine learning researcher and engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area. He builds at the intersection of deep learning, reinforcement learning and robotics, and the goal is usually to get algorithms to do something useful in the physical world. Rodolfo grew up in São Paulo, Brazil, and speaks Portuguese and English natively, plus some Spanish and French. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science (2025) and a B.A. in Computer Science with a concentration in Mathematics (2021), both from Columbia University, took graduate AI courses at Stanford through its Non-Degree Option, and has a high school technical degree in electronics from IFSP in Brazil.

## Current role at Pindrop

Rodolfo is a Research Scientist on the Authentication & ID Research team at Pindrop, a company that sells voice authentication and fraud detection models to banks. He has been in the role since June 2025. He works on the voice authentication models themselves, and with the bank teams that implement them and validate them for model risk, alongside Pindrop's sales engineers. It is his second time on the team: he was a Senior Data Scientist there from October 2022 to May 2024, left to finish his master's at Columbia, and was hired back into a research role. He also leads Pindrop's model risk management team and is a co-author of an ACL 2026 main-conference paper on bias in AI text detectors. During the master's he was a graduate research assistant in Columbia's Creative Machines Lab.

## Rodolfo's Pindrop articles

Rodolfo has two bylined articles on Pindrop's site. The first covers a Claude-based agent on AWS that drafts model development documentation; it cut the time per document from 25 days to 8. The second, "Automating Voice Migration from Existing Audio" (August 2026), covers voice migration: when a customer switches vendors, its existing call recordings become enrollments, so its callers don't have to enroll again. Rodolfo's offline enrollment work there went from proofs of concept to more than 3 million production enrollments across two migrations. The articles are at pindrop.com/resources/article/agentic-ai-for-model-development-docs and pindrop.com/resources/article/automating-voice-migration-from-existing-audio.

## Highlights and recognition

Rodolfo's main professional achievements are at Pindrop: he co-authored an ACL 2026 main-conference paper on bias in AI text detectors, built a Claude-based documentation agent that cut each model document from 25 days to 8, grew offline voice enrollment from proofs of concept to more than 3 million production enrollments, and leads the company's model risk management team. Academically, he finished his Columbia M.S. with a 4.036 GPA and received Columbia Computer Science's Behring Foundation Award in 2025. As an undergraduate he was a Science Research Fellow and made the Dean's List, and in high school in Brazil he won four national science olympiad gold medals, in physics, public-school physics, robotics and astronomy.

## What Rodolfo works on

Rodolfo's work spans three areas. In machine learning, he has built speaker and audio authentication models, diffusion models for visual planning, and reinforcement learning policies for legged robots. In robotics, he trained a quadruped to walk in simulation and moved the policy to real hardware, and built a perception and manipulation pipeline that tidies a workspace with a robotic arm. In hardware, he designs and builds his own devices end to end: CAD, 3D-printed parts, custom ESP32-S3 control boards, motor control firmware and smart-home integration over Matter. He also builds software for his own use, including two Flutter apps, RaiBudget and RaiClimbing.

## Rodolfo's interests and approach

Rodolfo's original training is in electronics, and his hardware projects, a motorized coffee grinder and two kinetic smart lamps, build on it. The grinder and the desk lamp are things he uses every day. His projects tend to close the loop between a simulation or a design and a working physical result, and he writes them up in detail, including the approaches that didn't work. In his LLM work, code computes the numbers, the model judges what they mean, a validator decides what reaches the user, and a person approves anything binding. Outside engineering, Rodolfo climbs, throws pottery on the wheel, hikes in US National Parks, and co-founded APOLE, a free math and physics program for public-school students in Brazil.
