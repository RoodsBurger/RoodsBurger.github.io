## Why hire Rodolfo

Rodolfo Raimundo is a good hire for teams that need machine learning research turned into systems people rely on. At Pindrop he built a Claude-based agent on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore that cut each model development document from 25 days to 8 and from 168 hours of work to 44, and it is now used by all of Pindrop's research teams. His offline voice enrollment work went from proofs of concept to more than 3 million production enrollments. He co-authored an ACL 2026 main-conference paper, leads Pindrop's model risk management team, and finished his Columbia M.S. with a 4.036 GPA. Pindrop hired him back into a research role after he left to finish the master's.

## Fit for LLM, agent and applied AI roles

Rodolfo builds LLM agents for production at Pindrop: a Claude-based documentation agent with a review agent that checks every number against the script that produced it, and a question-answering agent on Bedrock Knowledge Bases and Guardrails that cites a source for every answer and routes out-of-scope questions to the right team. He evaluates agents with regression sets and replayed edit sessions, and ran an A/B eval on his open-source Claude Agent Skill for circuit boards. In his own apps he keeps the model away from arithmetic and irreversible actions: code computes the numbers, the model interprets them, a validator gates the output, and a person approves anything binding. He also built this site's RAG chat assistant and co-authored an ACL 2026 paper on bias in AI text detectors.

## Fit for machine learning research roles

Rodolfo's research record covers speech, vision and robot learning. At Pindrop he built an authentication model on deep features from call audio, re-encoded with a multi-task contrastive network, that raised customers' authentication true positive rate by 2 percentage points, and he co-authored an ACL 2026 paper evaluating 16 AI text detectors for demographic bias. At Columbia's Creative Machines Lab he wrote a conditional diffusion model from scratch for visual planning in TidyNET. He also trained reinforcement learning policies for a quadruped and ran a neural network pruning study.

## Fit for robotics and hardware roles

Rodolfo takes robots from simulation to hardware. He trained Tobias, a quadruped he designed and 3D-printed, to walk with Soft Actor-Critic in PyBullet and ran the gait on the real robot. TidyNET, his system from Columbia's Creative Machines Lab, plans with a diffusion model, detects objects with an oriented-box YOLO model at over 93% detection success, and drives a WidowX 200 arm under ROS2 to 85.5% grasp success. He designs his own ESP32-S3 control boards, writes field-oriented motor control firmware, and ships Matter smart-home devices with signed over-the-air updates.

## Leadership and customer-facing work

Rodolfo leads Pindrop's model risk management team and its model risk committee, and writes the model documentation that bank validators review. He meets large US banks regularly, walks their model risk, compliance and IT teams through Pindrop's models, and works alongside Pindrop's sales engineers on implementations. At Next Caller he saved a customer that was about to leave by fixing its data integration and presenting the performance gain, which led to a $2 million contract renewal. He grew from Data Analyst to Senior Data Scientist to Research Scientist in the same company family. At the Columbia Space Initiative he was Zero-G Tech Lead on a payload that flew on Blue Origin's New Shepard, and he co-founded APOLE, which taught math and physics to more than 1,000 public-school students in Brazil.

## What Rodolfo is like to work with

Rodolfo is direct and understated, and he writes things down: his project write-ups include the approaches that didn't work and why. He builds end to end, from circuit boards and firmware to the app on top, and uses his own builds every day. He has taught and mentored students since he was 16. He is from São Paulo, Brazil, has worked in the US, Germany and Sweden, speaks Portuguese and English natively, and outside work he climbs up to V8 boulders, throws pottery and hikes US National Parks.
